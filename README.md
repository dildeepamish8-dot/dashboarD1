Built an end-to-end analysis of 97,000 delivered orders measuring fulfilment and revenue performance.
Median delivery time of 10 days (p90: 23 days), 6.8% late-delivery rate, and heavy revenue concentration: top 10 of 71 categories account for 60% of platform revenue.
Deployed a live, filterable Streamlit dashboard (state × category). Key finding: review scores fall from 4.46 for sub-3-day deliveries to 3.01 beyond 22 days, making delivery reliability the strongest satisfaction driver in the data.
